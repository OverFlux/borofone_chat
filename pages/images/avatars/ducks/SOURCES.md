# HITMAN duck avatar sources

These 16 avatar cutouts were adapted from item renders published on the
community-maintained HITMAN Wiki. Each image was background-removed, centered,
and exported as a 512×512 transparent PNG for Borotalk.

The underlying HITMAN designs and renders are the property of IO Interactive.
Their inclusion here does not grant redistribution or commercial-use rights.
Confirm permission before distributing Borotalk publicly.
